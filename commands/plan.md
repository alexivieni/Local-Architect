Turn this idea into a short spec: {args}

Write no code. Produce: one sentence saying what it is for and who uses it; the inputs and outputs; what is out of scope;
the open questions you could not answer from the project; and three to seven acceptance checks, each one something a test or a person
can verify with a yes or no. Read the existing code first so the spec fits what is already there. Keep it under one page. Do not invent
requirements the idea does not imply; list them as questions instead.
