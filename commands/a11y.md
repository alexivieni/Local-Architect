---
name: a11y
description: Fix what blocks keyboard and screen-reader users on the pages
flow: a11y
args: optional, a page or a folder of pages
---
Make the pages{args} usable with a keyboard and a screen reader.

Alt text on every image (empty for decoration), a label on every field, a name a screen reader can say on every button and link, real
buttons instead of clickable divs, a language and a title on every page, headings in order. Do not redesign anything: change only what
the fix needs, and say what you could not fix and why.
