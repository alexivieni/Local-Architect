Explain {args}.

Work it out from the code, not from what the names suggest. Use the tools: list, search, read. Name the files and line numbers you
relied on. Describe what happens in order, including the paths that are easy to miss: errors, empty input, concurrency.
If something looks wrong while you are reading it, say so at the end, separately from the explanation.
